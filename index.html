About this sandbox

Set an eruption and a wind field, watch the plume bend and the deposit drape over real terrain. The deposit is an analytical advection–diffusion solution with Reynolds-dependent settling, after Suzuki (1983) and the Tephra2 formulation; the falling particles are a Lagrangian view of the same equations, not decoration.

It is a teaching and exploration tool, not a forecast. The physics, the validation against an independent particle model, and the limitations that matter if you compare it with a real deposit are in the README.

Elevation: GSI 標高タイル (Geospatial Information Authority of Japan), zoom 12, resampled. Model after Suzuki (1983) and the Tephra2 formulation of Connor and colleagues; settling velocity after Bonadonna and colleagues (1998); plume height to eruption rate after Mastin and colleagues (2009); damage thresholds after Blong and colleagues (2017).
