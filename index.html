About this sandbox

Set an eruption and a wind field, then watch the plume bend and the ash settle over real terrain. The deposit on the map is an analytical advection and diffusion solution with settling speeds that depend on grain size, following Suzuki (1983) and the Tephra2 model. The moving particles follow the same equations one grain at a time.

It is meant for teaching and exploring. It does not forecast real eruptions. The README describes the physics, how the particles were checked against the deposit, and the limits to keep in mind when comparing with a real deposit.

Coordinates: JGD2011 / Japan Plane Rectangular CS II (EPSG:6670), 200 m grid. Elevation: GSI 標高タイル (Geospatial Information Authority of Japan), zoom 11, resampled. Deposit model after Suzuki (1983) and the Tephra2 model of Connor and colleagues. Settling velocity after Bonadonna and colleagues (1998). Plume height to eruption rate after Mastin and colleagues (2009). Damage thresholds after Blong and colleagues (2017).
